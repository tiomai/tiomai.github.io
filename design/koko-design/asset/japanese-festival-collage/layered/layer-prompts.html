Guidelines · layer-prompts

All six layers used the canonical Style A character sheet and the approved flattened collage as visual references. Each request specified one full-body character, a square canvas, genuine transparency, crisp Style A shapes, canonical colours, no text, no logo, no shadow, no additional character and no background decoration.

  • Koko: energetic jumping pose with one yellow uchiwa; blue monkey, peach heart-shaped face and ears, red cheeks and chest heart, curved blue tail, visible rounded triangular black nose, no shoes or shorts.
  • Gohanko: joyful pose with an unmistakable fish-shaped shoyu bottle; white body, black fringe and side buns, ochre hat, black bib, red torso circle, separate dark oval nose and yellow shoes. The bottle has a body, tail, fin, eye, brown soy fill and red mouth cap.
  • Rabbit: joyful pose with a pink-white-green dango skewer; pink body, long ears, black muzzle nose, forehead curl, paired cheek wedges, red scarf and no belly patch.
  • Bear: dynamic taiko pose with drum and two sticks; stocky brown body, pale muzzle, dark oval nose, angled brows, red cheeks, white lower garment, turquoise waistband and blue open happi vest.
  • Yauyau: floating waving pose; pointed charcoal hood, black face, white eyes and face mark, floating red sphere, tapered legless body and exactly two red scarf arms joined once to one neck band.
  • Tree: energetic pose with red maple leaf; grooved chestnut crown, peach face, upright rounded terracotta capsule nose, red cheeks, pink petal collar, green body and hands, pink legs and black shoes.

Gohanko and Bear required a second background-extraction pass because the first result contained a checkerboard as image pixels. The final files have actual alpha channels.
