Guidelines · README

A — Flat Adventure is selected for the landing page. This collection extends the same illustration language to News, Story, Chat, App and Store placements. Page assignments are proposals; no live pages have been changed.

Gohanko identity: a fish-shaped shoyu bottle with a red cap. Fish body, fins and tail must remain recognizable; a round bag is incorrect. Clothing may change to suit the activity, but facial marks and proportions stay fixed.

Guidelines · README

Page usage

  • Landing: use one lead scene with two or three supporting illustrations; do not put all 20 scenes on one page. Bookshop, market and shared study are evergreen leads. Festival scenes belong in seasonal slots.
  • News: community board and autumn observation can illustrate general discovery or category content; do not use fictional mascots as evidence for a real news event.
  • Story: riverside reading, tea, crafts and seasonal scenes support articles and learning collections.
  • Chat: market, soba, kissaten and shopping relate directly to conversation situations.
  • App/Store: train and shared study for learning on the go; furoshiki for book gifting.
  • Keep headlines, Japanese text, links and buttons as live UI. Blank areas are composition space, not a guarantee that text can overlay every crop. On mobile, stack copy and image.
  • These scenes use intentional clothing and objects to convey Japanese settings. Avoid reusing seasonal images year-round without contextual copy.

Files and method

The original PNG illustrations are generated with the built-in image tool. manifest.json contains all prompts, reference paths, proposed placement and cast. index.html is a local visual catalogue. Identity and technical inspection notes are recorded in QA.md. PNGs are flattened artworks, not editable layered masters or responsive exports.
