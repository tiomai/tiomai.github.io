Guidelines · IDENTITY-REVIEW

User corrections — required across all styles

  • Koko: one consistent filled black rounded triangular nose and short stem joining the rounded muzzle. Expression can change the mouth below; it must not replace the nose.
  • Gohanko: retain the small dark oval nose above the separate mouth, including smiling and closed-eye poses.
  • Rabbit: retain the small dark nose at the centre of the lower muzzle. The forehead curl is a separate feature above the eyes and cannot stand in for a nose.
  • Yauyau: exactly two scarf-arm attachments, left and right, each traceable to one hand. No third tail, branch or centre attachment.
  • Tree: retain a visible warm terracotta vertical rounded triangular nose, distinct from mouth and cheeks, following the 365 gym reference.

All prior A/D/E images are finish studies requiring identity cleanup; recording these rules does not repair those files. B/C remain outside the shortlist.

Guidelines · IDENTITY-REVIEW

E proportions — recommendation, not user approval

Prefer the compact source-aligned construction over the elongated E refinement. Do not intentionally compress the source either. Preserve each character's own head/body relationship, torso width and limb lengths across poses and styles. E's more stretched second version was an exploration to distinguish its editorial finish from B, not a canonical proportion change. Use E's finer outlines and elevated view with source-aligned bodies.

A camera change may reveal top surfaces and foreshorten visible forms. It must not be simulated by scaling artwork differently on horizontal and vertical axes. Establish a neutral front and three-quarter construction sheet first; measure ratios on that sheet rather than on seated/foreshortened mockups. No invented universal ratio across the six different characters.

Guidelines · IDENTITY-REVIEW

F — 365 Companion

New set: twelve poses, six plain and six with the same activity assignments used in A/D/E. Created with built-in image generation using native authored 365 cutouts 308, 311, 378, 384 and 385. Card 308 includes Koko and Rabbit. Card 377 was also inspected but not supplied to generation. Exact submitted prompt: prompt-F-365-final.txt.

Observed 365 rendering: warm medium-saturation fills, irregular clean edges, selective local-colour contour lines, small deliberate shadow/highlight patches and explicit character-specific facial marks. F is an approximation for comparison, not newly extracted authored artwork.

Visual review: all twelve poses are complete; noses are visible in both poses of Koko, Gohanko, Rabbit and Tree. Yauyau has two traceable scarf-arms in both poses. Main body proportions remain broadly consistent between the two rows. Source costume carryover remains: Koko has green shorts, Gohanko's round accessory became a soft bag, and Bear's lower garment gains a drawstring. These need normalization if F advances. This board is not a production-approved turnaround or layered master.
