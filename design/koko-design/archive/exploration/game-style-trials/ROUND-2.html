Guidelines · ROUND-2

User decision: keep A; B/C are not preferred. Their original files remain archived for history, but they are not recommendations in the active shortlist.

  • A — Flat Adventure: retained unchanged.
  • D — Colour Doodle: high-contrast identity colours, loose irregular silhouettes and selective coloured doodle lines; strong candidate for expressive editorial accents and playful scenarios. Background props can repeat the same irregular shapes and coloured marks, with fewer accents near reading content.
  • E — Outlined Isometric: flat outlined forms and elevated oblique view; candidate for scenes containing desks, rooms, streets and reusable environment modules. Use one shared camera convention for characters and props.

Both new boards compare six plain characters and the same six activities as A. This keeps the comparison focused on the finish. The references provide broad drawing qualities only; their text, branding and character identities are not part of the Kokomonster system.

Built-in image generation was used. Exact initial prompts and reference paths are in prompts-round-2.json. The first E draft was too similar to B, prompting a refinement toward smaller faces, longer limbs, fine contours and flatter fills. These are raster concept boards, not production vectors, animations or calibrated isometric assets.

D inspection: all twelve intended poses are present. Colour separation, irregular edges and selective doodle marks distinguish it from A. Some subtle tonal variation remains, and Rabbit has light inner-ear shapes requiring an explicit design decision. Preserve the source identity marks when refining the chosen direction. No landing-page or small-size validation is claimed.

E refinement inspection: all twelve poses are present, with thinner contours, longer limbs and a clearer elevated oblique view. The result is isometric-inspired rather than geometrically calibrated. Subtle shading remains; production should flatten fills. Gohanko's bag remains on the wrong side relative to the source convention, and Yauyau's leaf/hand contact needs cleanup. These do not prevent finish comparison but must be resolved before asset approval. Final refinement prompt: prompt-E-refinement.txt.
