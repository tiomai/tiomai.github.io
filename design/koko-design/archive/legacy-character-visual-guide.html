Guidelines · legacy-character-visual-guide

Source-of-truth order

  1. Approved master art in the Drive Card, Book-Story, and Design assets folders.
  2. Approved Figma components and exported production screens.
  3. Existing transparent PNG or layered illustration exports.
  4. Reference-assisted scene generation, only after identity details below are locked and an owner reviews the output.

Never redraw from memory when an approved master exists. Generated work must not replace the original model sheet or card artwork.

Koko — explorer and narrator

  • Dark navy monkey with a warm beige face and ears, red circular cheeks, small black eyes, and a red heart on the chest.
  • Active, curious, sociable poses: pointing, presenting, travelling, reading, discovering, or reacting.
  • Use for News, Stories, cultural discovery, and transitions between topics.
  • Keep Koko secondary to a real learner in lifestyle photography; a useful default is 12–22% of the human figure's visual height.

ご飯子 (Gohanko) — conversation guide

  • White rounded face and body, symmetrical black side-bun hair, straight black fringe, tiny black eyes and smile, red cheeks, black neck, red torso circle, tan rounded hat, and red-strap round bag.
  • Calm, reassuring poses: listening, inviting, prompting, or gently correcting.
  • Use for Chat and conversational support. Do not substitute Gohanko for Koko in adventure/news scenes without a specific narrative reason.
  • In a human-led composition, use at approximately 12–20% of the human figure's height. Avoid oversized mascot treatment on the main landing page.

Composition rules

  • Real person = emotional focus. Character = small guide or visual cue. Doodle = connection or direction.
  • Limit each composition to one primary character and one clear action.
  • Keep facial features, silhouette, signature colours, and props unchanged.
  • Avoid placing a character only to fill empty space. It should point, respond, introduce, or connect two pieces of content.
  • Do not crop through the face, signature hair/ears, heart, hat, or bag. Prefer full silhouettes or intentional waist-up framing.
  • Preserve a simple light direction and believable contact shadow when compositing into photography.
  • Use transparent approved assets over photos when possible. Never add generic square icon bases behind the characters.
Guidelines · legacy-character-visual-guide

Doodle language

  • Use 2–3 px hand-drawn lines in charcoal, Kokomonster purple, coral, or yellow.
  • Doodles can form arrows, paths, circles, underlines, stars, speech cues, and small sound marks.
  • Doodles should guide the eye; never trace over faces, important Japanese text, or product covers.
Guidelines · legacy-character-visual-guide

New-scenario workflow

  1. Complete an approved model sheet for each recurring character: front, three-quarter, profile, back, five expressions, and signature props.
  2. Record exact palette values, proportions, surface treatment, and prohibited changes.
  3. Export a small library of approved transparent poses before generating new poses.
  4. For a new scene, use an approved character export as an image reference and describe the action, camera, light, scale, and background separately.
  5. Compare the result against the model sheet at 100%: silhouette, face spacing, colour, props, and character role.
  6. Obtain brand-owner approval before a generated variant is used in production or advertising.

Reusable prompt skeleton

Place the referenced approved Kokomonster character in [specific action and setting]. Preserve the character exactly: [identity traits and signature props]. The character occupies [percentage] of the composition and acts as [narrative role]. Use [camera and lighting]. Add only [specific doodle cues]. Do not redesign, restyle, add clothing, alter proportions, change facial features, or invent a logo or text.

Remaining Drive audit

The deeper Card inventory should classify every asset by character, pose, expression, environment, card set, source format, resolution, transparency, and approval status.

Photoshop sources are present elsewhere in Drive, including Images_card_K.psd, Images_card_H.psd, Cards_好EASY.psd, 好Easy日本語_Mission.psd, and numerous page-layout PSDs. These are card and publishing compositions rather than the verified six-character master set. They can be inspected and exported layer-by-layer after being downloaded locally; extraction quality depends on whether character artwork remains on named, unmerged layers or is embedded as a Smart Object/flattened raster.
