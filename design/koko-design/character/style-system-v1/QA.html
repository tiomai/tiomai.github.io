Guidelines · QA

Limits visible in the current art

These are cleaned review mockups, not locked production model sheets. Koko's exact nose contour still varies subtly in A; facial placement and relative proportions are not mathematically identical across styles. C's Gohanko accessory remains on viewer-right in the source three-quarter sheet despite the requested side correction; side convention must be resolved against the original rotation during production tracing. B's small Tree nose has lower contrast than D's larger terracotta nose. These are explicit remaining normalization details, not approved variations.

All scene panels have the intended cast grouping: six in hero, two in reading, four in exploration. Faces remain visible and no third Yauyau scarf branch is apparent at board inspection size. Held objects hide some chest marks naturally. Gohanko accessory placement varies in scenes. Hero space is approximately a left third, with layout variations between treatments; it is a concept composition rather than a measured website slot.

No in-page/mobile readability, animation or conversion testing is claimed. Before publication, produce the chosen finish from shared editable construction masters, normalize the remaining nose/accessory geometry and test actual component sizes. The website itself remains unchanged.
