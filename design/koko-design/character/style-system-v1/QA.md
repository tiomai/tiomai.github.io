All scene panels have the intended cast grouping: six in hero, two in reading, four in exploration. Faces remain visible and no third Yauyau scarf branch is apparent at board inspection size. Hero space is approximately a left third, with layout variations between treatments; it is a concept composition rather than a measured website slot.

No in-page/mobile readability, animation or conversion testing is claimed. Before publication, produce the chosen finish from shared editable construction masters, normalize the remaining nose/accessory geometry and test actual component sizes. The website itself remains unchanged.
